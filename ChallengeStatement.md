# CHALLENGE 5 - 1435 Capital

Build a platform that aggregates public ecosystem signals to identify, map, and filter New Jersey's most promising startups.

## Problem Space:

Promising startups in New Jersey are hard to spot early because the signals that indicate momentum, such as funding activity, accelerator participation, hiring trends, and public support, are scattered across many different sources. Without a way to bring these signals together, it is difficult for investors, ecosystem builders, and economic development groups to identify which companies and sectors are gaining real traction across the state.

## What You'll Build:

A platform that can identify, map, and rank promising New Jersey startup opportunities by:

- Aggregating public and permissioned ecosystem signals, including funding activity, accelerator or incubator participation, intellectual property activity, hiring trends, etc.
- Organizing and breaking these signals into sector clusters
- Filtering these startups or sectors based on the strength and combination of signals detected

## Data & Resources:

- Public sources such as funding databases, patent and trademark filings, job posting platforms, and event or accelerator listings
- NJEDA grant and funding data, where publicly available
- Any permissioned or sample ecosystem data your team can reasonably access or simulate

## Deliverables:

- A working prototype that aggregates and displays ecosystem signals
- A ranking or scoring system for startups or sectors based on those signals
- A way to visualize sector clusters and trends across New Jersey
